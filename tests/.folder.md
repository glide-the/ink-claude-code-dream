Lifecycle fixtures emit readiness only after their signal handler and PID receipts exist; tests clean wrapper, core process group, and descendant in `finally`, including failed assertions. Tests do not make provider/model/OAuth/Remote calls and cannot claim official-core behavior or Dream business E2E.

`core-prune-contract.test.mjs` statically verifies the Bun `1.4.0` pin, explicit authorized source/package-root boundaries, ignored local-only output, digest-bound ripgrep plus Linux seccomp assets, the BPF-first 2.1.88 passthrough argv contract, reference-only nested-Docker Unix-socket exemption with candidate-environment removal, required capability roots, narrow MCP entry, deterministic resolver/facade controls, DCE assertions, and the source-bound provider-instance client memo.
